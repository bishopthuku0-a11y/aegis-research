## Bull vs Bear Debate

**The Bull Case:** The "Electrification Supercycle" is structural, not cyclical. AI data center power demand and the transition to renewables necessitate massive, multi-decade investment in grid hardening and transmission capacity. Utilities are "picks and shovels" plays for the AI revolution.

**The Bear Case:** Valuations have priced in perfection. Rising interest rates compress the P/E multiples of regulated utilities, and the grid equipment supply chain is hitting bottlenecks. Furthermore, if bipartisan political support for subsidized green-energy grid interconnection shifts, the massive CAPEX projections of the last 18 months may be revised downward, leading to a de-rating of high-multiple grid suppliers.

**The Challenge:** Bulls ignore the risk of regulatory lag—utilities cannot always pass through cost increases at the speed required to offset high borrowing costs. Bears ignore that grid load growth is currently exceeding even the most aggressive projections, creating a "must-spend" environment for utilities regardless of interest rates.
